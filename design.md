# Sky Print & Advertising Pvt. Ltd.

## Website Design Direction and Visual Design System

**Recommended direction:** Modern Corporate + Creative Print Studio

This document defines the recommended visual direction for the Sky Print
website. The goal is to create a professional, trustworthy, creative and
premium-looking website that reflects the company's printing and
advertising services.

The design should preserve the existing logo identity: blue represents
the sky and trust, orange represents creativity and energy, and the
printing-inspired colour details connect the brand to the printing
industry.

## 2. Design Patterns

The editorial grid should be the primary design language. The other
patterns should support it rather than compete with it.

### 2.1 Sun-ray geometry

**Decision: Keep**

Take inspiration from the orange sun rays in the actual logo.

-   Use refined, thin radial lines.
-   Apply them selectively in the hero background, decorative corners
    and section transitions.
-   Avoid large sunbursts repeated throughout the website.
-   Keep the pattern subtle and sophisticated.

### 2.2 Cloud-inspired curves

**Decision: Keep, but subtle**

Use smooth, thin blue curves inspired by the cloud shape above the SKY
lettering.

-   Use curves to frame content or create subtle section transitions.
-   Avoid cartoon clouds and large bubbly shapes.
-   Keep the treatment minimal and elegant.

### 2.3 Printing registration details

**Decision: Keep**

Use small details that connect the website to the printing industry.

Examples: - Crop marks - Alignment lines - Registration crosses - Small
CMYK-inspired colour indicators - Fine measurement or grid lines

Use these details sparingly as decorative elements, not as visual
clutter.

### 2.4 Editorial grid layout

**Decision: Make this the foundation**

Use a consistent editorial layout throughout the website.

-   Strong typography and clear hierarchy
-   Consistent spacing and alignment
-   Structured image galleries
-   Generous white space
-   Balanced content widths
-   Clean section transitions
-   Consistent card and image treatments

The editorial grid should control the overall layout. Sun rays, cloud
curves and printing marks should be secondary details.

## 3. Typography System

Use two complementary font families.

### Headings: Manrope

Recommended weights: `600`, `700`, and `800`.

Use Manrope for: - Hero headlines - Section headings - Service titles -
Important statements

Manrope provides a modern, geometric and confident appearance.

Example:

> Turning Ideas Into Impressions.

### Body and interface: Inter

Recommended weights: `400`, `500`, and `600`.

Use Inter for: - Paragraphs - Navigation - Buttons - Forms - Service
descriptions - Contact information

Inter provides strong readability for website content and interface
elements.

### Typography rules

-   Headings: Manrope, weights 600--800.
-   Body: Inter, weights 400--500.
-   Buttons: Inter, weight 600.
-   Maintain a consistent responsive type scale.
-   Avoid using too many font families.

## 4. Website Section Design Direction

### 4.1 Hero --- Cinematic and impactful

-   Use one continuous industrial printing video as the hero visual.
-   Keep the video visible as intended during the sticky scrolling
    experience.
-   Use a dark navy overlay only where necessary to maintain text
    readability.
-   Use a large white headline.
-   Use a primary blue CTA and restrained orange accent.
-   Keep the video as the visual centrepiece.
-   Avoid unnecessary decorative elements that compete with the footage.

### 4.2 About --- Clean and trustworthy

-   Use a white background.
-   Use deep navy headings.
-   Keep the company introduction concise and clear.
-   Use genuine company and printing photographs where available.
-   Avoid unsupported claims about company history, awards,
    certifications or achievements.

### 4.3 Services --- Structured and visual

-   Use a light-grey section background.
-   Use white service cards.
-   Include genuine service photographs where available.
-   Use restrained blue icons and small orange details.
-   Keep card layouts consistent.
-   Avoid giving every service card a different colour scheme.

### 4.4 Portfolio --- Let the work speak

-   Use a photography-led gallery.
-   Prefer large, clear images.
-   Use a clean and consistent grid.
-   Keep decorative elements minimal.
-   Let the actual printed designs provide the colour and visual
    interest.
-   Do not present sample or AI-generated visuals as actual company
    projects.

### 4.5 Footer --- Strong brand finish

-   Use a deep navy background.
-   Use white typography.
-   Add subtle cyan details.
-   Use orange for small highlights.
-   Keep contact information and navigation easy to find.

## 5. Design Decisions to Avoid

The following should be avoided in the website implementation:

-   Excessive blue-to-purple or pink gradients
-   Random neon colours
-   Glassmorphism everywhere
-   Glowing borders and glowing buttons
-   Oversized decorative sun graphics repeated in every section
-   Cartoon-style cloud illustrations
-   A different colour scheme for every service card
-   Excessive rounded corners on every element
-   Generic stock-looking or AI-generated printing equipment when
    genuine company photographs or video are available
-   Decorative effects that reduce readability or distract from the
    company's work

## 7. Final Design Principle

The website should look like an established professional printing and
advertising company, not a generic technology startup or an AI-generated
template.

The visual identity should be:

-   Professional enough for corporate clients.
-   Creative enough to represent advertising and design work.
-   Clear enough to communicate printing services.
-   Consistent with the existing Sky Print logo.
-   Simple and timeless rather than dependent on short-lived design
    trends.

Use the actual company logo as the source of truth for brand identity.
The HEX values in this document are the proposed website palette, not a
claim that every value is an exact extraction from the logo.

**Final recommendation: Approve the Modern Corporate + Creative Print
Studio direction and use this document as the visual design reference
before implementing the Next.js landing page.**
